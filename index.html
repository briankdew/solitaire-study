      How to play
      • Goal: build each suit from Ace up to the highest card on the foundations at the top right.
      • Foundations: one per suit, in the standard suit order ♠ ♥ ♣ ♦ from left to right (two suits are ♠ and ♥), each marked with an A and its suit while empty. Each takes only its own suit's Ace and then builds up in that suit; an Ace dragged onto any foundation goes to its own.
      • Shuffle, then deal: shuffling only mixes the cards, which wait face down in the stock with a Deal button on the last waste position. Dealing puts them on the table to start play.
      • Deal: the standard Klondike deal, in rows from the top of the deck: one card to each column from left to right with only the first face up, then a row starting at the second column, and so on, so the columns get 1, 2, 3… cards with only the last one face up. The rest of the deck becomes the stock in the same order, and its first cards (as many as one turn takes) are turned over so a card is playable right away.
      • Stock: click it to turn over more cards (as many as the Draw setting). The stock and waste work as one pile: once the stock runs out, it shows the refresh arrow while any waste cards are hidden under the fan, and clicking it turns the waste back over onto the stock and turns up the next cards in one go. When every remaining card is already showing, the stock is empty and clicking it just shakes the waste. Once one turn or less is left in the real stock, its top card shows how many cards remain. The waste has one dashed position per card drawn and always shows that many of its top cards; only the top (rightmost) one can be played, and playing it slides the others right and brings up the next card. There is no limit on passes.
      • Columns: build down in alternating colors, red on black or black on red. An empty column takes only the highest card, or a run that starts with it.
      • Click the top waste card or any face-up column card to play it automatically: to its foundation if it can go there, otherwise to the leftmost column that takes it. Cards on top of a clicked column card move with it, and a group like that only goes to a column. If a click sends a card to its foundation when you meant a column, click it again on the foundation straight away: it goes to the column instead, as if it had gone there in the first place (the record is corrected, no move is added, and Undo puts it back where it started).
      • Click a foundation's top card to bring it back down to the leftmost column that takes it (a new move, unless it is the take-back just described).
      • Drag any face-up card, with the cards on top of it, to a column or foundation. Cards on a foundation can be dragged back down to a column.
      • Once every card is face up, the game finishes itself, unless Auto-complete is turned off; then you play every card yourself. When you win, choose New deal to shuffle and deal a new game, Same deal to deal this shuffle again, or Quit.
      • Settings: the high card (the highest rank in each suit, 2 to K, with X for ten), suits (2 or 4), the number of columns (2 to 7) and cards drawn from the stock at a time (1 to 4), in the same order as a game record's header. Changing a setting shuffles a new deck for the new settings, with the same warning as New shuffle if a game is in play. There are always at least as many columns as suits, so every highest card can head a column; 4 suits also need a highest card of 3 or more. Column counts that would leave no cards for the stock are unavailable.
      • Custom shuffle: pick a numbered deal to shuffle the deck into exactly that order (shown left to right from the top of the deck), or Random. A deal also changes the other settings to the ones it is listed for, with Draw 3. The list has the 12 deals of the 4-card deck (high card 2, 2 suits, 2 columns), one for each game that is different once trading the two suits is set aside. A random shuffle that matches a listed deal shows its number.
      • Moves (numbered in the game record) count cards played to a column or foundation, including those the automatic finish plays. Turning the stock doesn't count.
      • Undo move takes back one action; it reads Undo draw when the last action was a turn of the stock. Restart deal deals the current shuffle again and starts play from the beginning. New shuffle mixes the cards without dealing; if a game is in play, it asks first. Quit stops play and leaves the table exactly as it is, until you choose Restart deal or New shuffle. End game does the same and records the game as a loss.
      • Pile names: the letters above the table name each pile for writing down play: s stock, w waste, h foundations, and a, b, c… the columns from left to right.
      • Game Record: the panel on the left shows the game as it is played, one step per action, newest at the bottom; scroll it to look back. Steps scrolled past either end stay pinned there, and the next step slides over them. Each line is cards and then a pile letter. D is the deal, in the order a physical deal shows it: each column's face-up card, then the waste cards showing. A numbered step is a move: the card moved (the bottom card of a run) and where it went, then what now shows on the pile it came from and that pile's letter (-- when it is empty). An unnumbered step is a turn of the stock, showing every waste card in view. Letters of your own moves are blue; the deal and the waste are gray. EOP ends a finished game: W (win) or L (loss, from End game) and the number of moves. Undo removes the last step.
      • Copy record copies the official record, the notation used to write a game down by hand: suit letters (S H C D), every item a card then its pile letter, a move written as one token (ADhACb, 2Dh--w), a stock turn naming the cards turned up (8S4H9Dw), and the result last (W7), under a Setup line of highest card, suits, columns and draw (e.g. 3223).
      • Keyboard: Tab to a card and press Enter to play it. Ctrl+Z undoes a move.

      Reshuffling will end the current game!

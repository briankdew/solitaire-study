    How to play
    • Goal: build each suit from Ace up to the highest card on the foundations at the top right.
    • Shuffle, then deal: shuffling only mixes the cards, which wait face down in the stock with a Deal button on the last waste position. Dealing puts them on the table to start play.
    • Deal: The columns get 1, 2, 3… cards with only the last one face up, and the first stock cards (as many as one turn takes) are turned over so a card is playable right away.
    • Stock: click it to turn over more cards (as many as the Draw setting), or to turn the waste back over when it runs out. Once one turn or less is left in the stock, its top card shows how many cards remain. The waste has one dashed position per card drawn and always shows that many of its top cards; only the top (rightmost) one can be played, and playing it slides the others right and brings up the next card. There is no limit on passes.
    • Columns: build down in alternating colors, red on black or black on red. An empty column takes only the highest card, or a run that starts with it.
    • Click the top waste card or any face-up column card to play it automatically: to the leftmost column that takes it, otherwise to its foundation. Cards on top of a clicked column card move with it. A clicked Ace always goes straight to a foundation.
    • Click a foundation's top card to bring it back down to the leftmost column that takes it.
    • Drag any face-up card, with the cards on top of it, to a column or foundation. Cards on a foundation can be dragged back down to a column.
    • Once every card is face up, the game finishes itself, unless Auto-complete is turned off; then you play every card yourself. When you win, choose New deal to shuffle and deal a new game, Same deal to deal this shuffle again, or Quit.
    • Settings: the highest card (2 to K, with X for ten), suits (2 or 4), the number of columns (2 to 7) and cards drawn from the stock at a time (1 to 4), in the same order as a game record's header. Changing a setting shuffles a new deck for the new settings, with the same warning as New shuffle if a game is in play. There are always at least as many columns as suits, so every highest card can head a column; 4 suits also need a highest card of 3 or more. Column counts that would leave no cards for the stock are unavailable.
    • Moves count cards played to a column or foundation. Turning the stock doesn't count.
    • Undo move takes back one action, including a turn of the stock. Restart deal deals the current shuffle again and starts play from the beginning. New shuffle mixes the cards without dealing; if a game is in play, it asks first. Quit stops play and leaves the table exactly as it is, until you choose Restart deal or New shuffle.
    • Pile names: the letters above the table name each pile for writing down play: s stock, w waste, h foundations, and a, b, c… the columns from left to right.
    • Game record: the strip under the table records the game as it is played, newest at the right, with a picture of each item above it. Every item is a card followed by its pile letter. The deal is written first: the waste cards turned up (playable card last), then each column's face-up card, e.g. 2♦2♣3♣w 3♦a A♦b. A move is the card moved (the bottom card of a run) and where it went, then the new top card of the pile it came from and that pile's letter, with -- when that pile is now empty: A♦hA♣b, 2♦h--w. Each turn of the stock is written like the deal's first item; turning the waste back over writes nothing. Undo removes the last item. Copy record copies the official record, with suit letters (S H C D) in place of symbols, under a Setup line of highest card, suits, columns and draw (e.g. 3223).
    • Keyboard: Tab to a card and press Enter to play it. Ctrl+Z undoes a move.

    Reshuffling will end the current game!

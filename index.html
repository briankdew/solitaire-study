How to play
  • Goal: build each suit from Ace up to 3 on the two foundations at the top right.
  • Stock: the first three cards are turned over when the game is dealt. Click the stock to turn the waste back over, then click again to turn three. Only the top card of the waste can be played. There is no limit on passes.
  • Columns: build down in alternating colors, red on black or black on red. An empty column takes only a 3.
  • Click the top waste card or any face-up column card to play it automatically: to the leftmost column that takes it, otherwise to its foundation. Cards on top of a clicked column card move with it. A clicked Ace always goes straight to a foundation.
  • Drag any face-up card, with the cards on top of it, to a column or foundation. Cards on a foundation can be dragged back down to a column.
  • Once every card is face up, the game finishes itself.
  • Keyboard: Tab to a card and press Enter to play it. Ctrl+Z undoes a move.

# Terminology (working draft)

The user's notes, kept exactly as written. First saved 2026-11-09 and updated the same day. They are not settled and have not been reviewed or adopted yet. They feed into the move classification (progressive, null, regressive) and the judgments in ROADMAP.md item 3.

```
terminology

play – move a card (or run, for moves from c)  (w-h, w-c, c-h, c-c, h-c)
p - play of 1 card (abbreviated notation)
pn - play of n cards (n ≥ 2 for run-plays)

reveal – make  a previously hidden card visible 
active – a card that is visible and selectable (available to be played)
lead   – the active (top) card in the waste pile
successor – a card that is visible in the waste pile but not active (by ordinal rank, e.g., 1st successor, 2nd successor)
promote – to make a successor active (by playing the lead)
hidden – a card that has not been revealed (in a column or the initial stock pile)
buried  – a lead or successor that has been covered (by a stock turn or waste recycle)
surface – to make a previously buried card active
recall – knowing  that a (and/or which) upcoming draw will surface a buried card
progressive – any action (play or draw) that objectively appears to be intended to allow play to continue
regressive – any action (play or draw) that objectively appears to be end or obstruct play from continuing
draw – any stock or (after the initial stock pile is depleted) waste turn whose aim is to reveal/surface a new lead on the waste pile
draw cycle – any cycle of draws that reveals every card in the initial stock pile or every card in a recycled waste pile
recycle – to turn over all cards in the waste pile so a new draw can be displayed
progressive draw – a draw that  reveals or surfaces a new lead
regressive draw – a draw that buries a playable lead
null draw – a draw that results in the same lead, or the first of a sequence of the same leads in the previous draw cycle
deal – the act of setting up the play area for initial game play (including the first draw of the game) from a freshly shuffled deck

p – progressive move (+n)
+2 - play to home (w-h) + unblock a play to home
+2 - play to home (h) + unblock a play from waste

n – null (0)
0 - no play
0 - no progress or regress

r – regressive (−n)
−1 - an h-c play with no benefit (marked with ‘?’)
−1a c-h play that prevents the play (or potential play) of a hidden or buried (in the waste or a column) card
−2 - removes a play from home + blocks a play from waste or column
−3 - removes a play from home + blocks multiple plays from waste or column
```
